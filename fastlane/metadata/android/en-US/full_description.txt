Pirate is a social music app for publishing, discovery, communities, live rooms, and chat.

Browse public and community feeds, vote and comment on posts, follow communities, manage your profile, listen to songs, and message people over XMTP.

Creators can publish text, image, video, link, song, and live-room posts. Song publishing supports original and remix attribution, while live rooms support scheduled solo or duet broadcasts.

Authentication is powered by Privy. You can also connect and link an external wallet to your Pirate account.
